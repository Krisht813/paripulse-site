Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Paripulse Management collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers the public website and the review workspace.

Do not send sensitive information. Keep credentials private. Never submit passwords, private keys, seed phrases, bank details or real payment information.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Paripulse Management is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact us at paripulse.support@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Account data: name, email address, account identifier, role and status.
  • Authentication data: sign-in identifiers, password hashes handled by Firebase Authentication, and security events such as password resets.
  • Workspace data: recorded wallet balances, workspace transactions, deposit requests, support tickets and administrative notes.
  • Audit and security data: actions performed in the dashboard, timestamps, account identifiers and role changes.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps and similar logs generated by our hosting, CDN and API providers.
  • Correspondence: information you send us by email, including the content of your message and any attachments.

3. Data we do not collect

We do not collect real payment card details, bank account details, government identity documents, real cryptocurrency private keys or seed phrases. We never ask for card numbers, bank credentials, private keys or seed phrases, and we do not store them.

The public contact form is sent to our API so that support can reply. It passes through our email provider, which processes the message on our behalf.

4. How we use personal data

  • to create and manage accounts and roles;
  • to operate the review dashboard and its recorded balances and records;
  • to provide support and respond to enquiries;
  • to detect, prevent and investigate security incidents and abuse;
  • to maintain audit logs and enforce access controls;
  • to comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms;
  • to improve the reliability and usability of the service.

5. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract or steps taken at your request; our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service; compliance with legal obligations; and consent where we specifically ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

6. Sharing and processors

We share data only where needed to run the service, including with:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud for authentication and database storage;
  • Cloudflare for the API, security and rate limiting;
  • GitHub Pages for website hosting;
  • Email providers that transmit and store support correspondence;
  • professional advisers, auditors or authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising networks or third-party analytics on this site.

7. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or an equivalent lawful transfer mechanism.

8. Retention

We keep account and workspace data while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to resolve disputes, maintain security records and meet legal obligations. Audit and security logs are kept only as long as needed for those purposes. Workspace data may be reset or deleted at any time.

9. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics cookies. Firebase Authentication may store sign-in state in your browser’s local storage or IndexedDB. Our hosting and security providers may set strictly necessary security cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from Google Fonts, so loading a page does not send your IP address to Google for font delivery.

You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings. Signing out and clearing site data removes locally stored workspace state.

10. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, audit logging, rate limiting and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure. If you believe your account or data has been compromised, contact us immediately and change your password.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to receive a portable copy, or to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise a right, email paripulse.support@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

12. Children

The service is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us so we can remove it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service, our providers or the law. The revised version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.

14. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: paripulse.support@gmail.com.
